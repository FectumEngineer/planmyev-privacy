PlanMyEv privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: PlanMyEv has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Everything you set up stays on your phone. The app only contacts the map, search, routing and charger services it needs to plan a trip.

Who this is for

PlanMyEv ("the app") plans electric-car road trips on Android: the route, where to charge, how long it takes and what it costs. It is provided by the developer, who can be reached through the contact link at the end of this page.

What stays on your phone

Everything you set up is stored only on your device: your cars and their figures, settings, saved trips, the trip you last planned, chargers you add or hide, and recent places you searched. The developer runs no server for the app, so none of this is uploaded to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

What is sent to other services

To work, the app contacts the services below. Each receives only what its job needs, together with your phone's IP address, as any internet request does.

ServiceWhyWhat it receives
Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)Find places by name, and turn your position into a place name The text you search for; the coordinates of your position when you tap "Use my current location"
OSRM routing serverWork out the driving route The coordinates of the start, the end and any places the route passes through
Open Charge MapFind charging stations The outline of your route, or the area around it, and the app's access key
MapTiler (with OpenStreetMap as a fallback)Show the map Requests for the map pieces (tiles) of the area you are looking at

These services have their own privacy policies: OpenStreetMap, MapTiler and Open Charge Map.

When you tap Open in Maps, the app hands your start, charging stops and destination to Google Maps, which then applies Google's own privacy policy.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap "Use my current location" in the place search, to set where a trip starts, and only while the app is open. It does not track you, does not use your location in the background, and does not keep your position (it is not added to your recent places).

Information from Google Play

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may give the developer anonymous crash and performance reports and install counts, as it does for any app. They do not identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can deny the location permission and still search for places by name. You can delete your saved trips, cars and chargers inside the app, or all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Substantial changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/FectumEngineer/planmyev-privacy/issues. Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
